Project 01

ABB GoFa CRB 15000 — 6-DOF Trajectory Planning & Kinematics

Closed-form analytical kinematics solvers and time-optimal bang-coast-bang trajectory optimisation for a 6-DOF collaborative arm, built in MATLAB and validated in RoboDK.

  • Closed-form forward and inverse kinematics — no iterative solver in the loop
  • Bang-coast-bang time law respecting per-joint velocity and acceleration limits
  • ~25% shorter cycle time with zero acceleration discontinuities

Project 02

ESP32 2.4GHz RF Spectrum Radar on CYD

Real-time Wi-Fi and RF channel spectrum inspector on the ESP32 Cheap Yellow Display's 320×240 TFT, driven by a custom SPI graphics pipeline. Published on Hackster.io.

  • Dual-core task execution: radio scanning on one core, rendering on the other
  • Direct hardware SPI rendering for a flicker-free live display
  • Signal-strength waterfall and per-channel congestion view
