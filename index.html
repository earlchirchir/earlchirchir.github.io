Trajectory Planning for an ABB CRB 15000 Robot Using Bang-Coast-Bang Time Law

Formulated closed-form analytical forward and inverse kinematic solvers, Jacobian velocity mappings, and time-optimal trapezoidal (Bang-Coast-Bang) velocity profiles. Validated multi-axis joint boundaries and collision-free paths through MATLAB and RoboDK 3D digital twin simulation.
